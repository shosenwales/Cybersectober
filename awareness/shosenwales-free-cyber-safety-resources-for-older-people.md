# Free cyber safety resources for older people

## Overview

This guide introduces AgbaWise (https://agbawise.com), a free website that teaches older Nigerians to recognize and avoid scams. It is an initiative by Cybernisoki, a Yoruba-language cybersecurity awareness platform.

The site has short lessons on how common scams work, followed by "What would you do?" practice using realistic messages and calls: fake bank alerts, "I changed my number" messages from a "son", WhatsApp code requests, fake grants, QR code tricks, and newer AI voice and video scams.

It is for parents, grandparents and older relatives, and for the younger family members who want to help them stay safe online.
## Prerequisites

None. All you need is a phone or computer with a web browser and an internet connection. There is no app to install, no account to create and no technical knowledge required. The site is built for mobile and written in plain English, so an older relative can use it alone or with a family member beside them.
